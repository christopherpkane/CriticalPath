Paste Test

Nothing opens by itself here and no link goes to Windows. Each button puts one thing on your clipboard. You paste it into a message you started yourself, the way the ones that work are sent, and press Send.

For each one:

  1. Press Copy here.
  2. In Phone Link, open a conversation with your own number.
  3. Click in the message box, press Ctrl+V, press Send.
  4. Come back and mark what happened.
